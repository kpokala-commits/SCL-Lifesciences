Built around your systems

Your ERP stays central. The view gets clearer.

Salesforce Tableau can bring selected information into simple, interactive views. With trusted definitions and the right access, people can see trends, ask why something changed and focus on what to do next.

How could this work technically?

Start with approved ERP and spreadsheet data. Establish a consistent definition for each metric, refresh it at an agreed frequency and make it available through Tableau. Tableau Pulse can surface changes and drivers; conversational analytics depend on the selected Tableau product, data model, access controls and entitlements.
