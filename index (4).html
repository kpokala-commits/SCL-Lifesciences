How it could work

The aim is to answer follow-up questions across products, orders and time using consistent cost definitions, rather than request a new fixed report for every question.

A sensible first scope: One product family and selected orders, using cost definitions agreed with Finance.

Technical boundary: Order, product and cost records must link reliably. Your team validates the reasons for any difference; Tableau does not set prices.

Build on what already works

Keep your systems.
Add a clearer view.

SCL has a homegrown ERP and a cross-functional CDMO project-management process. Add only the analysis or commercial controls that are missing.

Start with one product family or one commercial process. Expand only when it proves useful.

SCL's existing CDMO approach ↗

  • Existing systems keep their jobERP, batch, quality and project systems remain the source of operational records. No plant-control or batch-release replacement is proposed.
  • Tableau supports repeatable analysisShared metric definitions, interactive comparisons and access controls. Pulse can surface metric changes and drivers where configured.
  • Commercial workflow only where neededSalesforce opportunity management and approvals are an option if there is a confirmed gap around CDMO enquiries, quotes and commercial reviews.
Technical scope and sources

Start with approved exports or a supported data connection. Agree product and batch identifiers, the cost basis, refresh frequency and who may see what. These are configuration and data-model tasks, not benefits of a connector alone.

Tableau highlights statistical patterns; it does not prove physical causes. Production and quality decisions remain with authorised SCL staff. AI features, editions, consumption and deployment requirements need separate confirmation.
